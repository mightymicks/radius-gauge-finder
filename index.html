HOW TO CHECK IT
  1. Hold the UNDER blade against the part's radius. It should sit flush with no light gap — if it rocks or gaps, the part radius is larger than that blade.
  2. Hold the OVER blade against the same spot. You should see a slight light gap at the edges — if it sits perfectly flush too, the part radius may actually match the over blade.
  3. If UNDER is flush and OVER shows a gap, the part radius falls between the two — call it a pass against the nominal.
  4. If neither blade sits flush, the part radius is outside this bracket — flag it and remeasure.
